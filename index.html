Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026. This policy explains what data Nexa collects, why, and the rights you have over it.

DEMO MODE ACTIVE: Nexa is currently running in demo mode. All smart accounts, session keys, trading volumes, and portfolio balances are simulated. No real funds are moved or collected. This policy applies to both the demo and future live versions.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address, a hashed password (or your Google account identifier if you sign in with Google), and your chosen display name.
  • Public wallet addresses: the public addresses of wallets you connect or link, along with your wallet labels and network. We never collect private keys, seed phrases or signatures that could move funds.
  • App usage data: in-app activity records such as trades, swaps, transfers, positions, watchlist items and community posts — all simulated demo data during the beta.
  • Crash and diagnostic logs: technical error logs (such as error messages and page context you optionally send through the in-app feedback form) used to fix bugs.
  • Security data: identity-verification status, moderation reports and session records required to keep accounts and the community safe.

2. Passkeys and private credentials never leave your device

Your smart accounts (ERC-4337) are secured with biometric passkeys. Passkeys are generated on your device, stored in its secure hardware (such as Apple Keychain or Google Password Manager), and never leave your device. We never see, store or transmit your biometrics, passkeys, private keys, seed phrases or session keys.

We never sell your personal data, and we do not use your data to build advertising profiles.

3. How we use data

We use your data to provide the service (accounts, wallets, demo trading, community features), to keep accounts and the community secure, to fix bugs via crash and diagnostic logs, to communicate with you about your account, and to comply with applicable law. We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising.

4. Sub-processors

We rely on the following third-party providers to operate Nexa:

  • Supabase — account authentication and database hosting for your account, wallets and app data.
  • ZeroDev — smart account (ERC-4337) infrastructure and gas sponsorship for wallet operations on test networks.
  • News feed providers (such as CoinDesk, Decrypt, MarketWatch, CNBC, BBC, The Guardian and Al Jazeera) — public market news displayed in the app. Headlines are fetched server-side and not attributed to you personally.

Each provider processes data only as needed to deliver its function, under its own privacy terms. Our current providers are listed here and will be updated if this changes.

5. Your rights (GDPR and CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you have rights over your personal data, including under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Access and portability — request a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data, including your email and display name.
  • Erasure — delete your account and data (see below).
  • Objection and restriction — object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Non-discrimination — exercise your rights without being treated differently (CCPA).

To exercise any right, contact us at support.nexa.omni@gmail.com. We respond within the timelines required by applicable law.

6. Data export and account deletion

Data export: request an export of your account data at support.nexa.omni@gmail.com and we will provide it in a machine-readable format.

Account deletion: you can permanently delete your account in the app under Security & Privacy. Deleting your account removes your account, wallets, trades, positions, messages, posts and settings, signs you out, and clears local data on your device. This self-service deletion meets app-store requirements and is the fastest way to erase your data.

7. Retention and security

We keep your data only as long as your account is active or as required by law. After account deletion, personal data is removed or irreversibly anonymized. We protect data in transit and at rest, enforce least-privilege access (row-level security), and require strong authentication (hashed passwords, optional two-factor, passkeys) — see our Security & Privacy page.

8. Children's privacy and contact

Nexa is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Privacy inquiries: support.nexa.omni@gmail.com. See also our Terms of Service and Fees page.
